Two IIT Bombay batchmates, Dhruv Jaglan and Georgi Boby, launched Rilo in September 2025. This week Adobe acquired it, less than a year after launch.

Rilo let non-technical teams describe a workflow in plain English and have AI agents execute it across hundreds of tools. It scaled to 10,000 users within months, and last raised a $1 million seed from Peak XV Partners and DeVC at a $10 million valuation.

The catch: the platform shuts down and the team joins Adobe. Deal value undisclosed. It is Adobe's second Indian acquisition after Rephrase.ai in 2023.

Founder takeaway: ship narrow, ship fast, and build exactly where a giant has a gap.
