How does Waymo turn AI into ~$355M in annualized revenue without a human driver?

17 years of driving, training, simulation and safety testing, city by city. A fast layer (cameras, LiDAR, radar) sees and reacts. A slow layer (a vision-language model trained using Gemini) reasons about unusual roads. A Driver, Simulator and Critic loop keeps it learning, and big Teacher models are distilled into small, fast Student models that actually drive.

Waymo didn't just put AI in a car. It built a system that can see, understand, decide, act and learn. That's the principle we build on at Minutrix: business context → AI → execution. Link in bio.

Sources: Sacra (revenue, rides), Waymo blog (architecture, London).
