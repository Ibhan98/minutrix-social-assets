What if AI wasn't adapted for India, but built from it?

That is Sarvam AI's bet. The Bengaluru startup trains its own models for India's languages, runs a full stack from speech to agents, and already handles 2M+ conversations and about 1 crore API calls a day across government, finance, aviation and agriculture.

The bigger lesson for anyone building with AI: the winners may not build for everyone first. They may understand one market better than anyone. (Figures: Sarvam via TechCrunch, June 2026; Government of Maharashtra; IndiGo. Minutrix is not affiliated with Sarvam AI.) Link in bio.
