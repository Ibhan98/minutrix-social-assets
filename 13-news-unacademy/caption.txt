Unacademy was valued at $3.4 billion in 2021. On 1 September 2026 it sold to upGrad for about $206 million, in an all-stock deal. That is roughly 94% below its peak.

Co-founder Gaurav Munjal confirmed the close. The term sheet was signed in March and the Competition Commission of India cleared the deal in July.

The founder lesson is not "edtech is dead". It is that a valuation is a snapshot, and growth bought with burn always comes due. Know your runway date before the market picks it for you.
