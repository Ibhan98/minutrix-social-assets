7 AI buzzwords, decoded.

Agentic AI. MCP. RAG. Function calling. Orchestration. Vector databases. Multi-agent systems. Every "AI-powered" pitch this year uses at least three of these. Here is what each one actually means, in one slide each, and how they fit together.

The short version: function calling lets a model act. MCP standardises how it plugs into your tools. RAG and vector databases give it your data. Agents plan and loop over those actions. Orchestration and multi-agent systems make several of them work as one.
