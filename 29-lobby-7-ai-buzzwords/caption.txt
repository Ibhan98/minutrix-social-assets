7 AI buzzwords you'll be hearing a lot more about.

Agentic AI, MCP, RAG, function calling, orchestration, vector databases, multi-agent systems. Every pitch this year leans on at least three of them. Most people in the room can't define one. After this carousel, you can.

The short version: function calling lets a model act, MCP gives it one plug into your tools, RAG and vector databases give it your data, agents plan and loop, orchestration and multi-agent systems make several of them work as one.
