Building AI without an eval set is shipping blind.

Before we write a prompt, we write thirty examples. Thirty real inputs, including the ugly ones, each with the answer a good person would give. That is the spec. It turns "is it good?" into "27 of 30", and it scores every future prompt, model and price change.

Models change every quarter. A good eval set is how you know in ten minutes whether to switch.
