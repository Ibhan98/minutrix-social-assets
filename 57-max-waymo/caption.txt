17 years. One robotaxi company. Zero drivers. 🚗

Waymo's playbook, collage edition: cameras + LiDAR to see, a Gemini-trained model to reason, a simulator + critic to keep learning off the road, and big "teacher" models distilled into small, fast "student" models that do the actual driving.

The lesson isn't "add AI". It's build a system that sees, decides, acts and learns. That's what we do for businesses at Minutrix. Save this one. Link in bio.
