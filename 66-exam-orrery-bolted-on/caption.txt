Bolted on or woven through? Four tests, thirty seconds.

Delete the AI feature and nothing changes: bolted on. The model lives in a chat box, not in the defaults: bolted on. The user stops doing nothing new: bolted on. The prompt is one untouched file: bolted on.

Woven through means the product is different because the intelligence is there. Quieter, faster, fewer decisions.
