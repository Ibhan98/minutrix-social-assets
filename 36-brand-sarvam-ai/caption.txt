India is building its own AI. Here is who is doing it.

Sarvam AI is a Bengaluru startup founded in 2023 by Vivek Raghavan and Pratyush Kumar. It trains language, speech and document models from scratch for Indian languages, released Sarvam 30B and 105B as open weights in 2026, and became a unicorn in June with a $234M round led by HCLTech.

Why it matters for business: a customer who calls in Tamil, or switches to Hinglish mid-sentence, can now be answered by AI in the same language.
