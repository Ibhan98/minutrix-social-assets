Why can AI give a completely wrong answer while sounding 100% sure?

It's called a hallucination: AI makes up a fact, a statistic, a source or a quote and presents it as real. Five things cause it: knowledge gaps, imperfect data, lost context in long chats, vague prompts, and a confident tone that hides all of it.
