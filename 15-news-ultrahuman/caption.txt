Bengaluru-based Ultrahuman, the smart ring company, raised $70 million this week: $65 million in equity and $5 million in debt. Investors include Qualcomm Ventures, Labcorp, Alpha Wave, Blume, Nexus and Alteria.

The round values Ultrahuman at $365 million, about 3x its $120 million valuation in 2023.

Where it goes: sensing, AI, miniaturised electronics and health algorithms, plus Pulsomics, its opt-in clinical research engine running studies on VO2 max, sleep needs and glucose metabolism. The stated ambition is to move from wearable brand to a human-computer interface built around the body.

Founder takeaway: Indian hardware can raise big when the data moat is real, and the company that names the category usually owns it.
